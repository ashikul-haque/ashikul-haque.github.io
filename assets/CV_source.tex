\sect{Research Interests}

\bitem \textbf{Internet of Things (IoT) and Wireless Networked Systems:} anti-jamming, coexistence, and satellite connectivity for low-power wide-area networks (LPWANs).
\bitem \textbf{Real-Time and Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS):} real-time scheduling for physical artificial intelligence (AI), from large language model (LLM) agents to connected vehicles.
\bitem \textbf{AI for IoT and CPS:} reinforcement learning and generative AI for networked systems.

\sect{Research Highlights}

Nine peer-reviewed conference papers, eight as first author, at venues including ACM \venue{SenSys}, IEEE \venue{RTSS}, IEEE \venue{INFOCOM}, and ACM \venue{MobiHoc}, seven of them at conferences with acceptance rates from 14\% to 25\%. Published the first anti-jamming work for LPWANs and built gateway-side decoders that raise packet reception under jamming by up to 106.56\x{} with no change to the LoRa end devices. Published the first work that enables communication from Long Range Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum (LR-FHSS) to LoRa, which improves reliability at long range by up to 79\%. Designed deadline-aware token scheduling for LLM agents that control physical devices, which cut the share of critical requests that were rejected or late from 92.5\% to 23.8\% in a vLLM stress test.
